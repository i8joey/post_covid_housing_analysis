Post-COVID US Housing Market Analysis

Overview
An analysis of the US housing market from 2019 to present, 
examining how key economic indicators correlated with the 
post-COVID housing price surge and subsequent correction.

Data Sources
All data pulled from FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data):
- **MSPUS** — Median Sale Price of Houses in the US
- **MORTGAGE30US** — 30-Year Fixed Mortgage Rate
- **JTSJOL** — Job Openings (JOLTS Report)
- **UNRATE** — Unemployment Rate

Tools
MySQL, Python (pandas)

Key Findings
- 2021 saw the largest single quarter price increase,
  coinciding with historically low mortgage rates and surging job openings
- 2023 saw the highest price drop, aligning with aggressive Fed 
  rate hikes pushing mortgage rates above 7%
- Job openings peaked in 2022 before declining alongside home prices
